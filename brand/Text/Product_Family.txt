ATLAS GEOGRAPHIC RELEASE CONVENTION

Series = a larger geographic region, island, state, country, or related area.
Version = a city, district, town, village, or locality within that Series.

Example:
🌊 Long Island Series
Atlas 1.0 — Long Beach
Atlas 1.1 — Island Park
Atlas 1.2 — Baldwin
Atlas 1.3 — Garden City

Future Series can draw from New York, Florida, the Caribbean, and other places associated with the GoldenSun identity.
