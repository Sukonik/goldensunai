GoldenSun AI ☀️

GoldenSun AI is an AI-native product studio focused on building practical software for understanding information, environments, and everyday decisions.

Built by Team GoldenSun, the company combines human product leadership with specialized AI agents across product strategy, design, research, engineering, and development. GoldenSun AI explores consumer software, local-first AI, legal technology, environmental intelligence, AI orchestration, and next-generation personal computing.

Current and emerging projects include ClearSky, Ari Planner, Capsule AI, Q-Lens, BlackBowAI, and Golden Sun Atlas.
