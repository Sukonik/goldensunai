SHORT ABOUT

GoldenSun AI is an AI-native product studio building practical software across local AI, legal technology, environmental intelligence, personal planning, and AI computing.

Our products are built by Team GoldenSun: a human-led development team that collaborates with specialized AI agents for product design, research, engineering, testing, and iteration.

LONGER ABOUT

GoldenSun AI builds focused software that helps people understand information, navigate their environment, and make better decisions.

We are developing a growing family of products spanning weather and environmental intelligence, personalized planning, legal document technology, local AI tooling, and portable AI computing. Our development model combines human product judgment with specialized AI agents that contribute to research, design, engineering, testing, and rapid iteration.

Rather than treating AI as a feature added at the end of development, GoldenSun AI explores what happens when AI becomes part of the product team itself.
